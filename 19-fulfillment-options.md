## Comparison Table

| Option | Cost/Order (Single Shirt) | Cost/Order (2-Pack) | Onboarding Time | Capacity | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A: Ship direct from VA printer** | $3-5 (shipping only) | $4-6 | None (existing relationship) | Unknown — depends on printer's shipping setup | Fastest path. No middleman. Lower cost. | Printers aren't fulfillment experts. May bottleneck at high volume. No rate discounts. |
| **B: ShipBob** | $5-8 (pick/pack + shipping) | $6-9 | 2-4 weeks | High (national warehouse network) | Professional 3PL. Shopify integration. Multi-warehouse for faster delivery. | Expensive. 15-30% shipping markup. 3% credit card surcharge. Onboarding takes weeks. |

## Shipping Cost Estimates (USPS/UPS)

| Method | Single Shirt (8oz pkg) | 2-Pack (14oz pkg) | Delivery Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| USPS First Class | $3.50–$4.50 | $4.50–$5.50 | 3-5 days |
| USPS Priority Mail | $7.00–$9.00 | $8.00–$10.00 | 2-3 days |
| UPS Ground | $6.00–$8.00 | $7.00–$9.00 | 3-5 days |
| USPS Priority (ShipStation discounted) | $5.50–$7.00 | $6.50–$8.00 | 2-3 days |

**Recommendation:** Use **USPS First Class** for single shirts (cheapest, fast enough if shipped by July 21) and **USPS Priority** for bundles/late orders where speed matters.

## Recommendation: Phased Approach

### Phase 1 (Orders 1–20,000): Ship from Virginia Printer
- **Why:** Fastest to start. No onboarding. No middleman.
- **How:** Printer prints shirts → packs in poly mailers → generates USPS labels via ShipStation → ships direct
- **Requirement:** Printer must agree to handle pick/pack/ship. Discuss capacity limits upfront.
- **Estimated cost:** $3-5/order (shipping label only, since printing is already happening there)

### Phase 2 (Orders 20,000+): Add 3PL if Needed
- **Why:** If printer can't keep up with shipping volume, offload fulfillment
- **How:** Printed shirts trucked to a nearby Virginia/East Coast 3PL (ShipMonk has an NJ facility)
- **Trigger:** Printer shipping > 500 orders/day and quality or speed is slipping
- **Estimated cost:** $5-7/order (3PL pick/pack + shipping)

### Why NOT Start with a 3PL?
- **Onboarding takes 2-4 weeks** — eats into the tight timeline
- **Adds a transit step** — printer → truck → 3PL → customer (vs. printer → customer)
- **Adds cost** — 3PL markup on top of shipping
- **The printer already has the shirts** — it's the most efficient to ship from where they're printed

---

## Key Actions

1. **This week:** Ask Virginia printer if they can handle fulfillment (ship direct to customers). Get their capacity limit per day.
2. **If yes:** Set them up with ShipStation for label generation and tracking sync back to Shopify.
3. **If no (or capacity < 500/day):** Begin ShipMonk onboarding immediately as backup.
4. **Order poly mailers** (10" × 13" for single shirts, 12" × 16" for bundles) — ~$0.10-0.15 each in bulk.
