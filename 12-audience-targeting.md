## 2. Behavioral Audiences

- Online shoppers (Meta pixel data)
- Recent t-shirt / apparel purchasers
- Engaged shoppers (people who click "Shop Now" on Meta ads frequently)
- People who RSVP to movie events on Facebook
- Recent app activity on Fandango or AMC apps

## 3. Demographic Filters

| Parameter | Setting | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Age** | 18–45 | Core range. Focus budget on 25–35 for nostalgia angle. |
| **Gender** | All | Couples/family angle means both genders equally valuable |
| **Location** | United States | Domestic shipping only |
| **Language** | English | — |
| **Placements** | Instagram Feed, Instagram Stories/Reels, Facebook Feed, Facebook Reels | Mobile-first. Skip Audience Network. |

---

## 4. Lookalike Audience Strategy

### Phase 1 (Pre-launch, Week 1-2): No Lookalikes Available
- Run interest-based audiences only
- Collect pixel data aggressively (ViewContent, AddToCart, Purchase events)

### Phase 2 (Week 3-4): Build First Lookalikes
- **1% Lookalike of Purchasers** — Once 100+ purchases, create this. Highest quality.
- **1% Lookalike of Add-to-Cart** — Available sooner (lower threshold). Good bridge audience.
- **1% Lookalike of Website Visitors (60s+)** — People who spent time on the site. Available fastest.

### Phase 3 (Week 5+): Scale Lookalikes
- **3% Lookalike of Purchasers** — Broader reach, slightly lower quality
- **5% Lookalike of Purchasers** — Maximum scale, use for broad awareness
- Stack Lookalikes with interest targeting for refined audiences

## 5. Retargeting Funnel

| Stage | Audience | Window | Message | Expected CPA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **RT1** | Viewed product page, didn't add to cart | 3 days | "Still thinking about it? Opening night is coming." | $3–5 |
| **RT2** | Added to cart, didn't purchase | 3 days | "Your shirt is waiting. Don't miss opening night without it." | $2–4 |
| **RT3** | Engaged with ad (liked, commented, shared) but didn't visit | 7 days | Different creative angle — try couples/family bundle | $4–6 |
| **RT4** | Purchased — upsell | 7 days | "Your crew needs matching shirts" — bundle offer | $3–5 |
| **RT5** | All site visitors, no purchase | 14 days | Urgency: "Only X days until July 31. Order now for guaranteed delivery." | $4–7 |

### Urgency Escalation (Last 2 Weeks Before July 31)
- RT windows tighten to 1–2 days
- Messaging shifts to "Last chance" / "Final orders ship [date]"
- Budget shifts 40% to retargeting (vs. 20% during testing phase)

---

## 6. Exclusions

| Exclude | Why |
|---|---|
| Existing purchasers (from upsell campaigns) | Don't show them the same product they already bought |
| People under 18 | Apparel purchase decisions — focus on adults |
| International audiences | US shipping only |
| People who hid/reported your ad | Wasted spend, signals negative sentiment |
| Employees / team members | Don't inflate metrics |

---

## 7. Testing Structure

### Week 1-2: Discovery
- 5 interest-based ad sets × 3 creatives each = 15 ads
- $50/day per ad set = $250/day total
- Goal: Find which audiences and creatives have lowest CPA

### Week 3-4: Validation
- Kill ad sets with CPA > $8
- Scale winners to $100-200/day
- Launch Lookalike audiences
- Add retargeting funnel
- $500-1,000/day total

### Week 5-6: Scale
- Top 3 audiences at $500-2,000/day each
- Lookalikes at $500-1,000/day
- Retargeting at 20-40% of total budget
- $5,000-15,000/day total (ramp toward $15K/day in final week)
