## Can We Hit $6 CPA?

### The Math

$6 CPA requires either:
- **Very low CPC + high conversion:** $0.45 CPC × 7.5% conversion rate = $6 CPA
- **Moderate CPC + good conversion:** $0.60 CPC × 10% conversion rate = $6 CPA

### Reality Check

| Factor | Assessment |
|---|---|
| $0.45 CPC | Achievable — apparel benchmark. Strong creative can hit this. |
| 7.5–10% conversion rate | **Very aggressive.** Typical cold-traffic Shopify conversion is 2–4%. |
| Event-driven urgency boost | Countdown timers, limited-time offers, and FOMO can push conversion higher |
| Warm/retargeted traffic | Retargeting audiences convert 3–5x higher than cold. A blended CPA of $6 is more realistic than cold-only $6. |

### Verdict

**$6 blended CPA is ambitious but achievable** if:
1. Creative is strong (high CTR drives down CPC)
2. Retargeting funnel is well-built (high-converting warm traffic lowers blended CPA)
3. Landing page converts well (mobile-optimized, single-product focus)
4. The Brand New Day hype provides organic tailwind

**$6 cold-traffic-only CPA is unrealistic.** Plan for $8–12 cold, $3–5 retargeting, blending to ~$6 overall.

## Key Benchmark Warnings

1. **CPMs are rising year-over-year across all industries.** Every industry saw increases in 2025. Budget for 10-15% CPM inflation.
2. **Summer 2026 will be competitive.** Avengers: Doomsday (May), Toy Story 5 (June), Brand New Day (July) — expect higher CPMs in entertainment/pop-culture audiences.
3. **New ad accounts have lower trust scores.** Expect higher costs in the first 2 weeks as Meta's algorithm learns.
