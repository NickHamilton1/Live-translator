# Shopify Store Blueprint

---

## Landing Page Structure (Mobile-First)

### Above the Fold
1. **Hero image** — High-quality mockup of the #1 shirt on a model. Lifestyle shot (couple at movie theater, group of friends). NOT a flat product image.
2. **Headline:** "Opening Night Deserves Opening Night Merch"
3. **Countdown timer** — Days/hours/minutes until July 31, 2026
4. **CTA button:** "Shop Opening Night Shirts" → scrolls to product grid

### Product Grid
5. **Featured collection** — Top 5-6 designs in a clean grid. Large product images. Price visible.
6. **Bundle callouts** — "Couples Set — Save 14%" and "Family Pack — Save 23%" highlighted with badges
7. **Quick-add buttons** — Size selector + add to cart without leaving the page

### Social Proof / Trust
8. **"As seen on" or review count** — Even early on, show order count: "500+ pre-ordered"
9. **Urgency bar** — "Pre-orders close [date]. Guaranteed delivery before July 31."
10. **Sizing chart** — Expandable, visual (not just a table). Include model measurements.

### Below the Fold
11. **The Story** — "Three eras. 24 years. One opening night." Brief emotional copy about growing up on superhero movies.
12. **Bundle section** — Dedicated area showing couples and family packs with lifestyle imagery
13. **FAQ** — Shipping times, return policy, sizing help, "when will I receive my shirt?"
14. **Email capture** — "Get notified when new designs drop" — even if they don't buy, capture the lead

## Product Page Best Practices

- **4-6 mockup images** per product: front, back (if applicable), on model, lifestyle context, close-up of print, size comparison
- **Sizing chart** accessible in 1 tap — modal overlay, not a separate page
- **One-line description** above the fold, detailed description below
- **Scarcity messaging:** "Limited print run — no restocks"
- **Delivery estimate:** "Order by [date] for guaranteed arrival before July 31"
- **Mobile: product images should be swipeable**, full-width

---

## Checkout Optimization

- **One-page checkout** (Shopify's default since 2024 — ensure it's enabled)
- **Upsell at cart:** "Complete the set" — if they add a single shirt, suggest the couples/family bundle
- **Express checkout:** Apple Pay, Google Pay, Shop Pay. These reduce friction massively on mobile.
- **Free shipping threshold:** "Free shipping on orders over $38" (the couples bundle price — nudges single-shirt buyers to add a second)

## Mobile-First Design Priorities

80%+ of Meta ad traffic will be mobile. Prioritize:

1. **Page load speed < 3 seconds** — Compress images, minimize apps, use Shopify's default theme (Dawn) or a fast lightweight theme
2. **Thumb-friendly CTAs** — Add to Cart button sticky at bottom of screen on mobile
3. **Product images fill the screen** — No wasted whitespace on mobile product pages
4. **Minimal navigation** — Landing page should convert without the user ever touching the nav menu
5. **Express checkout visible** — Apple Pay / Shop Pay buttons above regular checkout
6. **No pop-ups on mobile** — Kills conversion. Use a non-intrusive slide-up for email capture.
