# Top Designs for Production & Ad Testing

Ranked from the 15 concepts. Criteria: emotional resonance, legal safety, broad appeal, uniqueness vs. competitors, meme potential, design simplicity.

---

## Tier 1: Lead with These (Highest Confidence)

**#1 — "Since 2002"** (Concept #1)
Strongest nostalgia play. Simple, clean, universally understood by the target demo. Zero legal risk. Easy to produce. This is the anchor shirt.

**#2 — "Opening Night Crew"** (Concept #2)
Event-driven urgency. No competitor sells this. Creates FOMO — you need it BEFORE July 31 or it's pointless. Best for ad hooks with countdown timers.

**#3 — "I Remember Everything"** (Concept #3)
The emotional gut-punch. Fans of No Way Home will feel this instantly. Minimal design = cheap to produce, looks premium. Strong potential for organic social sharing.

**#4 — Couple Bundle "The Love Story"** (Bundle A: Concept #7)
The biggest gap in the market. No one is selling superhero-movie couples shirts. Highest AOV potential. Strong ad creative angle ("date night at the movies").

**#5 — Family Bundle "Heroes Raising Heroes"** (Bundle D: Concept #9)
The generational story is the entire business thesis. Parents who grew up on Tobey bringing kids to Brand New Day. Emotional, shareable, unique.

---

## Tier 2: Strong Supporting Designs

**#6 — "Three Eras, One Story"** (Concept #4)
Appeals to the "all three are great" majority. Clean design with the three years. Good for group buys (friend groups each repping an era).

**#7 — "24 Years in the Making"** (Concept #15)
Timeline design has visual appeal and works in ad carousels. Tells a story at a glance. Pairs well with "Since 2002" for variety.

**#8 — "No Spoilers"** (Concept #11)
Humor sells. Front/back design is shareable on social media. Universal appeal — works for any movie, not just Spider-Man. Good for organic/meme-style ad creatives.

**#9 — Couple Bundle "Across Every Universe"** (Bundle B: Concept #8)
The emotional version of the couples set. Slightly higher risk (Yellow) but more powerful emotional hook. Test against Bundle A.

**#10 — "Day One Fan"** (Concept #6)
Simple, bold, wearable beyond the event. The "loyalty badge" shirt. Works year-round.

---

## Tier 3: Test & Learn

**#11 — "Bitten by the Bug"** (Concept #5)
Strong concept but Yellow risk due to spider + "bitten" combo. Test in ads — if it sells well, keep it. If it draws attention, pull it fast.

**#12 — "Still Here. Still Fighting."** (Concept #13)
Emotional but more niche. Appeals to fans connecting with Brand New Day's isolation theme. Good for retargeting (show to people who engaged with other ads but didn't buy).

**#13 — "Evolve"** (Concept #12)
Trendy, design-forward, appeals to younger demo (18-25). Yellow risk but completely generic imagery. Good for Instagram/TikTok ad creative.

**#14 — "The Web We Weave"** (Concept #14)
The most "wearable" design — could be worn as a fashion piece, not just event merch. Lower conversion potential from cold ads but good for organic/social.

**#15 — Family Bundle "Family Movie Night"** (Bundle E: Concept #10)
The most versatile family set — works for any movie night, not just this one. Lower urgency but broader appeal. Test against the "Heroes Raising Heroes" bundle.

---

## Ad Testing Plan

- **Phase 1 (Week 1-2):** Test Tier 1 designs (#1-5) with $50/day each across 3 audiences
- **Phase 2 (Week 3-4):** Add Tier 2 winners, kill losers from Tier 1
- **Phase 3 (Week 5+):** Scale winners, test Tier 3 as creative refresh
- **Kill criteria:** Any design with CPA > $8 after $200 spend gets paused
- **Scale criteria:** Any design with CPA < $5 gets 2x budget immediately
