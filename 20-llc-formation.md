# Delaware LLC Formation

---

## Step-by-Step with Costs and Timeline

### Step 1: Choose a Name & Check Availability (Day 1, 15 min)
- Search Delaware Division of Corporations: [icis.corp.delaware.gov](https://icis.corp.delaware.gov/ecorp/entitysearch/namesearch.aspx)
- Name must include "LLC" or "Limited Liability Company"
- Pick something generic to the business (e.g., "Hero Night Apparel LLC") — not tied to Spider-Man IP
- **Cost:** Free

### Step 2: Appoint a Registered Agent (Day 1, 15 min)
- Required: a physical address in Delaware to receive legal documents
- **Recommended services:**
  - Northwest Registered Agent — $125/year (includes free filing assistance)
  - Incfile / ZenBusiness — $100-150/year
  - Harvard Business Services — $50/year (Delaware-based, long-standing)
- **Cost:** $100-150/year

### Step 3: File Certificate of Formation (Day 1, 30 min)
- File online at [corp.delaware.gov](https://corp.delaware.gov/howtoform/)
- **State filing fee:** $90
- **Expedited processing options:**
  - 24-hour: +$50 ($140 total)
  - Same-day: +$100 ($190 total)
  - 2-hour: +$500 ($590 total)
  - 1-hour: +$1,000 ($1,090 total)
- **Recommendation:** Same-day for $190 total. No reason to wait.
- **Cost:** $190 (same-day)

### Step 4: Draft Operating Agreement (Day 1-2, 1 hour)
- Not filed with the state but legally essential
- For a single-member LLC: use a template (available free from Northwest, ZenBusiness, or LegalZoom)
- Key provisions: management structure, profit distribution, dissolution terms
- **Cost:** Free (template) or $100-300 (attorney-drafted)

### Step 5: Get an EIN from the IRS (Day 1-2, 15 min)
- Apply online at [irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online)
- **Instant** — EIN issued immediately upon completing the online form
- Required for: bank account, Shopify Payments, tax filing
- **Cost:** Free

### Step 6: Open a Business Bank Account (Days 2-5)
- **Online options (fastest):**
  - Mercury — online application, 1-2 business days approval, no fees
  - Relay — similar to Mercury, free plan available
  - Novo — free business checking, Shopify integration
- **Traditional banks:** Chase, Bank of America — may require in-person visit, takes 3-7 days
- **Required documents:** Certificate of Formation, EIN confirmation, Operating Agreement, government-issued ID
- **Cost:** Free (online banks) or $0-25/month (traditional)

### Step 7: Connect to Shopify Payments (Day 3-7)
- In Shopify admin → Settings → Payments → Shopify Payments
- Enter: business legal name, EIN, business bank account info, business address
- Shopify Payments (powered by Stripe) — 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction on Basic plan
- **Approval:** Usually instant to 2-3 business days
- **Cost:** No setup fee (transaction fees apply per sale)

## Important Notes

1. **Delaware vs. your home state:** If you live outside Delaware, you may also need to register as a "foreign LLC" in your home state. This adds $100-300 and another filing. Consult an accountant.
2. **Delaware franchise tax:** $300/year minimum, due June 1. Budget for this.
3. **Separate finances:** Keep business and personal finances completely separate. Commingling funds is the #1 way courts pierce the corporate veil (see `04-risk-scenarios.md`).
4. **Business credit card:** Apply for one after the bank account is open. Recommended for Meta ad spend to build business credit and keep finances separate.
