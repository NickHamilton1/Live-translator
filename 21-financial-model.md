## Break-Even Analysis

### What CPA Kills Profitability?

At $22 sell price and ~$9 COGS (base case):

| CPA | Profit/Unit | Break-Even? |
|---|---|---|
| $4 | $9.06 | Very profitable |
| $6 | $7.06 | Profitable |
| $8 | $5.06 | Thin but viable |
| $10 | $3.06 | Marginal — needs high volume |
| $12 | $1.06 | Barely viable — bundles only |
| **$13.00** | **$0.06** | **Break-even (before overhead)** |
| $14+ | Negative | **Unprofitable** |

**Break-even CPA: ~$13/unit** (before overhead/legal costs)
**Practical break-even CPA: ~$11/unit** (including overhead allocation at 50K units)

### What Sell Price is Needed at Different CPAs?

| CPA | Min Sell Price (for $5/unit profit) |
|---|---|
| $6 | $20 |
| $8 | $22 |
| $10 | $24 |
| $12 | $26 |

## Cash Flow Considerations

### Upfront Capital Required

| Expense | When | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| LLC formation | April | $500 |
| Legal review (attorney) | May | $3,000–$5,000 |
| Shopify + apps | June 1 | $50/month |
| Initial blank shirt order (10K units) | June 1 | $22,000–$35,000 |
| Meta ads — testing phase | June 16 | $4,200 |
| Second blank order | June 21 | $22,000–$66,000 |
| Meta ads — validation phase | July 1 | $11,200 |
| Third blank order | July 7 | Variable |
| Meta ads — scale phase | July 14 | $216,000 |
| **Total upfront before significant revenue** | — | **$80,000–$150,000** |

### When Does Money Come In?
- Shopify Payments: payout within 2-3 business days of each sale
- Pre-orders (June 16+) generate revenue immediately
- Cash flow turns positive within 1-2 weeks of pre-orders opening if volume is decent
- **Key risk:** Ad spend ramps faster than revenue in the scale phase. Need $50K-100K in available credit/capital to bridge the gap in late July.
