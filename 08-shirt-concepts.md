# Shirt Design Concepts

**Legal basis:** All concepts are Green or Yellow per `03-design-guardrails.md`. No trademarked names, character likenesses, or protected phrases.

---

## Solo Fan

**1. "Since 2002"**
- **Category:** Era-Specific / Nostalgia
- **Text:** "SINCE 2002" in bold vintage block letters
- **Visual:** Distressed retro typography, faded red/blue color scheme, small original spider icon beneath the year
- **Target:** 28-40 year olds who saw the first film as kids
- **Risk:** GREEN

**2. "Opening Night Crew"**
- **Category:** Simple Slogan / Event
- **Text:** "OPENING NIGHT CREW — JULY 31, 2026"
- **Visual:** Movie ticket stub graphic with popcorn bucket. Retro marquee lettering. No character imagery.
- **Target:** Anyone going to see Brand New Day opening night
- **Risk:** GREEN

**3. "I Remember Everything"**
- **Category:** Solo Fan / Emotional
- **Text:** "I REMEMBER EVERYTHING"
- **Visual:** Clean white text on black shirt. Minimal. Possibly a single small web-line accent.
- **Target:** Fans of No Way Home's memory-wipe ending. Emotional resonance.
- **Risk:** GREEN — generic phrase, no IP reference

**4. "Three Eras, One Story"**
- **Category:** Era-Specific / Nostalgia
- **Text:** "THREE ERAS. ONE STORY. 2002 · 2012 · 2017"
- **Visual:** Three vertical stripes (red, blue, black) behind the text. Clean modern typography.
- **Target:** Fans of all three Spider-Man actors. The "I love them all" crowd.
- **Risk:** GREEN

**5. "Bitten by the Bug"**
- **Category:** Humor-Meme / Nostalgia
- **Text:** "BITTEN BY THE BUG" with small text underneath: "and never recovered"
- **Visual:** Simple original spider silhouette (NOT Marvel's logo). Vintage comic-book halftone dot pattern background.
- **Target:** Lifelong fans. Double meaning — bitten by a spider / bitten by fandom.
- **Risk:** YELLOW — spider imagery + "bitten" is evocative but all elements are original. Keep the spider design clearly distinct from Marvel's.

**6. "Day One Fan"**
- **Category:** Simple Slogan
- **Text:** "DAY ONE FAN"
- **Visual:** Bold sans-serif type, small "est. 2002" underneath. Red shirt, white text.
- **Target:** OG fans who want to signal their loyalty
- **Risk:** GREEN

## Couple Sets

**7. "My Hero" / "My Universe"**
- **Category:** Couple Set
- **Text:** Shirt A: "YOU'RE MY HERO" / Shirt B: "YOU'RE MY UNIVERSE"
- **Visual:** Complementary designs — Shirt A in red with small web-line heart accent, Shirt B in blue with star/galaxy accent. Matching typography.
- **Target:** Couples going to the movie together. Date night energy.
- **Risk:** GREEN

**8. "I'd Forget the World for You" / "I'd Find You in Any Universe"**
- **Category:** Couple Set / Emotional
- **Text:** As above — one shirt per partner
- **Visual:** Minimalist white text on black shirts. Small spider-web heart graphic connecting the two designs when standing side by side.
- **Target:** Couples who connect with the Peter/MJ memory-wipe storyline
- **Risk:** YELLOW — clearly evokes the NWH storyline, but all words are generic. No names, no IP.

## Family Packs

**9. "Raised on Heroes" Family Set**
- **Category:** Family Pack
- **Adult shirt:** "WE RAISED THE NEXT GENERATION OF HEROES"
- **Kid shirt:** "MY PARENTS WERE HEROES FIRST"
- **Visual:** Retro badge/crest design with a small original spider icon. Matching color palette (navy blue base).
- **Target:** Parents (30-40) bringing kids to opening night
- **Risk:** GREEN

**10. "Family Movie Night" Set**
- **Category:** Family Pack
- **Adult shirt:** "FAMILY MOVIE NIGHT — EST. [customizable year]"
- **Kid shirt:** "THE SEQUEL" or "THE NEWEST FAN"
- **Visual:** Popcorn bucket, movie ticket stubs, theater seats. Fun illustrated style. No character imagery.
- **Target:** Any family going to any blockbuster — versatile beyond just Spider-Man
- **Risk:** GREEN

## Humor / Meme

**11. "No Spoilers"**
- **Category:** Humor-Meme
- **Text:** "NO SPOILERS" on front. Back: "I HAVEN'T SEEN IT YET" (pre-release version) or "I JUST SAW IT — ASK ME NOTHING" (post-release version)
- **Visual:** Bold stencil typography. Caution-tape yellow and black color scheme.
- **Target:** Fans going to early screenings. Plays on Tom Holland's "spoiler king" reputation without naming him.
- **Risk:** GREEN

**12. "Evolve"**
- **Category:** Humor-Meme / Trending
- **Text:** "EVOLVE" in distorted, glitching typography
- **Visual:** Abstract metamorphosis imagery — cocoon cracking open, organic shapes. Dark color palette (black/dark red).
- **Target:** Fans hyped about the Man-Spider transformation teased in the trailer
- **Risk:** YELLOW — clearly connected to Brand New Day's plot, but "evolve" and metamorphosis imagery are completely generic.

## Simple Slogan

**13. "Still Here. Still Fighting."**
- **Category:** Simple Slogan / Emotional
- **Text:** "STILL HERE. STILL FIGHTING."
- **Visual:** Minimal white text on dark shirt. Optional: small spider-web texture in the background, barely visible.
- **Target:** Fans connecting with the isolated, lonely Peter from the Brand New Day trailer
- **Risk:** GREEN

**14. "The Web We Weave"**
- **Category:** Simple Slogan / Artistic
- **Text:** "THE WEB WE WEAVE"
- **Visual:** Intricate geometric spider-web illustration as the main design element. Text integrated into the web pattern. Original art — not Marvel's web style.
- **Target:** Design-forward fans who want something wearable beyond opening night
- **Risk:** GREEN — webs are generic, phrase is original

**15. "24 Years in the Making"**
- **Category:** Era-Specific / Event
- **Text:** "24 YEARS IN THE MAKING — 2002–2026"
- **Visual:** Timeline graphic with three dots at 2002, 2012, 2017 leading to a large "2026" endpoint. Clean infographic style.
- **Target:** Nostalgia buyers, the "I was there for all of it" crowd
- **Risk:** GREEN
