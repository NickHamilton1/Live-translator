## Pricing Logic

| Type | Single Shirt | Bundle Price | Discount | Margin Per Unit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Single | $22 | — | — | ~$7.50 |
| Couple (2 shirts) | $44 | $38 | 14% | ~$6.50/shirt |
| Family 3-pack | $66 | $52 | 21% | ~$5.50/shirt |
| Family 4-pack | $88 | $68 | 23% | ~$5.00/shirt |

Bundles sacrifice ~$1-2.50/unit margin but increase AOV (average order value) significantly. A $68 family pack order is 3x the revenue of a single shirt, even at lower per-unit margin. Meta ads cost the same CPA regardless of order size.

## Upsell Strategy

- **At cart:** If someone adds a single shirt, show the couple bundle as "Add your partner's shirt — save 14%"
- **At checkout:** "Make it a family night — add kids' sizes for $14 each" (discounted from $22)
- **Post-purchase email:** "Your crew needs matching shirts" — retarget with bundle offers
