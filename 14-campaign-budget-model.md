## Campaign Hierarchy

```
ACCOUNT LEVEL
└── Campaign 1: PROSPECTING (Advantage+ Shopping)
│   ├── Ad Set: Interest — Spider-Man / Marvel fans
│   ├── Ad Set: Interest — Movie culture / Fandango / AMC
│   ├── Ad Set: Interest — Nostalgia / Millennial
│   ├── Ad Set: Lookalike — 1% Purchasers (Week 3+)
│   └── Ad Set: Lookalike — 3% Purchasers (Week 5+)
│
└── Campaign 2: RETARGETING (Manual / Advantage+)
│   ├── Ad Set: RT1 — Viewed product, no ATC (3-day)
│   ├── Ad Set: RT2 — ATC, no purchase (3-day)
│   ├── Ad Set: RT3 — Engaged with ad, no visit (7-day)
│   └── Ad Set: RT4 — Purchasers — upsell bundles (7-day)
│
└── Campaign 3: BROAD / SCALE (Week 5+, Advantage+)
    └── Ad Set: Broad targeting (age 18-45, US only, no interests)
        — Let Meta's algorithm find buyers with minimal constraints
```

## Total Budget Model

| Phase | Duration | Daily Spend | Phase Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Testing | 14 days | $300 | $4,200 |
| Validation | 14 days | $800 | $11,200 |
| Scale | 18 days | $12,000 | $216,000 |
| Post-release | 7 days | $5,000 | $35,000 |
| **TOTAL** | **53 days** | — | **$266,600** |

**At $6 CPA, $266K buys ~44,400 customers.** To reach 100K units, need higher AOV (bundles averaging 1.5+ units/order) or lower CPA.

**At $6 CPA and 1.5 units/order average: 44,400 orders × 1.5 = 66,600 units.** Still short of 100K.

**To hit 100K units at $6 CPA:** Need $600K total ad spend (100K ÷ 1.0 if all single orders). At 1.5 units/order average, need $400K.

## Can a New Meta Account Handle This Volume?

### Spending Limit Challenges
- **New accounts start with low daily spending limits** — typically $50-250/day
- **Business verification** is required to unlock higher limits (takes 1-5 business days)
- **Spending limits increase gradually** as Meta trusts the account
- **Never increase daily budget by more than 20% at a time** — resets learning phase

### Ramp-Up Reality
| Week | Realistic Daily Spend | Cumulative |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | $100-300 | $1,400-2,100 |
| Week 2 | $300-500 | $3,500-5,600 |
| Week 3 | $500-1,000 | $7,000-12,600 |
| Week 4 | $1,000-3,000 | $14,000-33,600 |
| Week 5 | $3,000-8,000 | $35,000-89,600 |
| Week 6 | $8,000-15,000 | $91,000-194,600 |

**Realistic maximum from a new account over 6 weeks: ~$150K-$200K**

### How to Get to $600K
1. **Start Meta account setup NOW** (April) — verify business, build trust, run small test campaigns before June
2. **Use an existing Business Manager** if available from your current merch business
3. **Multiple ad accounts** under one Business Manager (Meta allows 5+ accounts per BM)
4. **Request spending limit increases** proactively through Meta support

## CPA Scenario Decision Tree

| Blended CPA | Action | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| **$4 or below** | 🟢 Scale aggressively. Max budget. Add designs. | Printing money. Every dollar spent returns $3+. |
| **$5** | 🟢 Scale. Increase budget 20% every 3 days. | Strong ROI. Push toward maximum volume. |
| **$6** | 🟡 Proceed as planned. Monitor closely. | On target. Profitable but thin if COGS rise. |
| **$8** | 🟡 Optimize before scaling. Test new creatives. | Still profitable ($5.50/unit) but margin pressure. Cut underperforming ad sets. |
| **$10** | 🟠 Pause scaling. Focus on retargeting and bundles to increase AOV. | $3.50/unit profit — only works at high volume. Need AOV above $30. |
| **$12** | 🔴 Reduce spend to retargeting only. Reassess strategy. | $1.50/unit profit. Not worth scaling. Focus on existing warm audiences. |
| **$14+** | 🔴 Pause all prospecting. Sell through existing pipeline only. | Unprofitable at single-shirt AOV. Only viable if bundles dominate. |
