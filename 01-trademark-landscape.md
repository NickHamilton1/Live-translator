# Spider-Man Trademark & IP Landscape for Apparel Business

## 1. IP Ownership Structure

The Spider-Man intellectual property is split across multiple entities in one of the most complex rights arrangements in entertainment:

| Rights Category | Owner | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Character IP (copyright & trademark)** | Marvel Characters, Inc. (subsidiary of Marvel Entertainment / The Walt Disney Company) | Owns the underlying character, name, likeness, and all trademarks |
| **Comic books** | Marvel Comics (Disney) | Full creative and publishing control |
| **Film rights** | Sony Pictures (Columbia Pictures) | Acquired from Marvel in 1998 for ~$7 million. Sony must continue producing films to retain these rights |
| **MCU co-production** | Marvel Studios + Sony Pictures | Sony retains distribution and ownership; Marvel Studios co-produces (Homecoming, Far From Home, No Way Home, Brand New Day) |
| **Television / animation** | Marvel Entertainment (Disney) | Disney+ animated series "Your Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man" (2025) |
| **Merchandise & consumer products** | The Walt Disney Company (via Disney Consumer Products) | Sony sold merchandising rights back to Disney in 2011 |
| **Video games** | Complex -- licensed by Marvel/Disney to partners | Sony has exclusive console deals (Insomniac's Spider-Man games), but Marvel controls the underlying license |
| **Streaming (films)** | Sony Pictures | Sony controls streaming windows for Spider-Man films independently of Disney+ |

### Key Takeaway for Apparel

**Disney/Marvel controls all merchandise licensing, including apparel.** Sony's film rights do NOT extend to t-shirts, clothing, or consumer products. Any legitimate Spider-Man apparel license must come through Disney Consumer Products (formerly Marvel Consumer Products).

## 2. The Marvel/Sony Merchandise Rights Split -- History

- **1998:** Marvel, in financial distress, sold Spider-Man film rights to Sony Pictures for approximately $7 million.
- **1999-2010:** Sony held both film and certain merchandising rights tied to the films.
- **2011:** Sony sold merchandising rights for Spider-Man back to Disney/Marvel. Sony's electronics business was under severe strain, and the company prioritized liquidity over long-term merchandising revenue.
- **2015:** Marvel Studios and Sony struck a deal to share Spider-Man on screen (MCU collaboration), but merchandise rights remained firmly with Disney.
- **Present (2026):** Disney controls 100% of Spider-Man consumer products licensing -- toys, apparel, costumes, backpacks, collectibles, and all physical/digital merchandise.

**Practical implication:** Even though Sony co-owns the films and controls distribution, if you want to put Spider-Man on a t-shirt, you deal with Disney, not Sony.

## 6. What This Means for a T-Shirt Business

### What is protected (DO NOT use without a license)

- The word "Spider-Man" in any spelling (Spider-Man, Spiderman, Spider Man)
- The spider logo/symbol in any version (chest emblem, back emblem, web designs associated with the character)
- Character likenesses (the costume, the mask, the eyes, web-shooters)
- Movie titles ("No Way Home," "Brand New Day," "Homecoming," "Far From Home")
- Taglines ("With Great Power Comes Great Responsibility" -- explicitly registered in Class 025 for shirts)
- Character names ("Peter Parker," "Miles Morales," "Gwen Stacy" / "Spider-Gwen")
- Associated brand names ("Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man," "Ultimate Spider-Man," "Amazing Spider-Man")
- The "Spidey" nickname
- Web-pattern designs closely associated with the character

### How to get a legitimate license

1. **Contact Disney Consumer Products** via [disneystudiolicensing.com](https://www.disneystudiolicensing.com/) or email permissions@marvel.com
2. **Requirements are substantial:**
   - Minimum ~5 years of business history typically required
   - Expected to generate six-figure annual royalties (implies $1M+ in product sales)
   - Royalty rates typically 15%-30% of wholesale
   - Non-exclusive license for a specific product category, territory, term, and distribution channel
   - Legal fees for negotiating a comparable licensing deal can exceed $150,000
3. **Smaller alternatives:**
   - Print-on-demand platforms (Merch by Amazon, Redbubble, TeePublic) sometimes carry officially licensed Marvel designs through their own licensing agreements
   - Authorized wholesale distributors carry pre-licensed Spider-Man apparel for resale

### Risk of unlicensed use

- Marvel/Disney is one of the most aggressive trademark enforcers in the world
- Etsy, Amazon, and other platforms routinely remove listings for unauthorized Spider-Man merchandise
- Civil penalties for trademark infringement can include statutory damages, actual damages, and disgorgement of profits
- Criminal penalties for willful counterfeiting can include fines up to $2M and imprisonment

## 7. Recommended Next Steps

1. **Search USPTO TESS directly** at [tmsearch.uspto.gov](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/) for the most current Spider-Man filings, especially:
   - "SPIDER-MAN BRAND NEW DAY" (may have been filed in late 2025 or early 2026)
   - Any new Class 025 filings tied to the upcoming film
2. **Consult a trademark attorney** before using any Spider-Man-adjacent designs, names, or visual elements
3. **Contact Disney Consumer Products** if pursuing a legitimate licensing arrangement
4. **Consider parody/fair use limitations** -- these are extremely narrow for commercial merchandise and generally do NOT protect t-shirt designs that trade on the character's goodwill
