# Ad Creative Strategy

---

## Ad Formats (Ranked by Priority)

1. **Static image** — Shirt mockup on model or flat-lay. Fastest to produce. Best for testing.
2. **Short-form video (6-15s)** — UGC-style: person unboxing, holding up shirt, reacting. Strong on Reels/Stories.
3. **Carousel** — Show multiple designs or the couples/family bundle story across slides.
4. **Meme-style static** — Text-heavy, looks organic, blends into feed. Low production cost.
5. **UGC compilation** — Once you have early customers, compile reaction videos.

---

## Hook Categories

| Hook | Emotional Lever | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **Nostalgia** | "Remember when..." / "We grew up on this" | 25-35 demo, Solo fan shirts |
| **Urgency** | "Opening night is in X days" / countdown | Everyone, last 2 weeks |
| **Couples** | "Date night just got an upgrade" | Couples bundles |
| **Family** | "The whole family is going" | Family packs |
| **FOMO** | "Don't be the only one without it" | Cold audiences |
| **Humor** | Meme formats, "No Spoilers" energy | 18-30 demo, viral potential |

## Testing Methodology

| Parameter | Approach |
|---|---|
| **Creatives per ad set** | 3 (Meta recommends 3-6 for Advantage+ optimization) |
| **Budget per test** | $50/day per ad set for 4 days = $200 minimum per test |
| **Kill threshold** | CPA > $8 after $200 spend → pause |
| **Scale threshold** | CPA < $5 after $200 spend → increase budget 20% |
| **Creative fatigue signal** | CTR drops 30%+ from peak → swap creative |
| **Testing cadence** | Launch new batch of 3-5 creatives every 5-7 days |
| **Total creatives needed** | 20-30 over the 6-week campaign (launch 5, test 15-25 more) |

## Creative Production Plan

### Week 1 (Pre-launch): Produce 10 Creatives
- 5 static mockups (shirt on model via Placeit/Printful mockup generator)
- 3 meme-style statics (Canva — text-heavy, feed-native)
- 2 short-form videos (iPhone footage of shirts, unboxing style)
- **Cost:** $0-200 (mockup tools + Canva Pro)

### Week 2-3: Produce 10 More Based on Winners
- Double down on winning hooks (if nostalgia wins, make 5 more nostalgia variants)
- Add carousel formats for bundles
- Shoot UGC-style video if possible (send shirts to 2-3 friends/family, film reactions)

### Week 4-6: Refresh Cycle
- Replace fatigued creatives
- Add urgency overlays to existing winners ("Only 10 days left!")
- Compile any customer photos/UGC for social proof ads
