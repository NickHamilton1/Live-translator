## Recommended Product: Gildan 5000

- **Weight:** 5.3 oz (mid-weight, durable enough for event wear)
- **Material:** 100% preshrunk cotton
- **Colors available:** 70+ (focus on Black, White, Navy, Red, Royal Blue)
- **Sizes to stock:** S, M, L, XL, 2XL (skip 3XL+ initially — low demand, high inventory risk)
- **Why this shirt:** Cheapest at volume, universally available, prints well with DTG and screen print, good enough quality for event merch that doesn't need to last years

## Procurement Strategy

### Wave 1 (June 1-7): Initial Stock
- Order 10,000 blanks based on estimated size/color mix
- Split: 50% Black, 20% White, 15% Navy, 10% Red, 5% Grey
- **Cost at $2.20/unit: $22,000**
- Ship directly to Virginia printer

### Wave 2 (June 21): Demand-Based Reorder
- Analyze first 5 days of pre-order data
- Reorder based on actual demand (design × size × color)
- Order 10,000-30,000 additional blanks
- **Cost: $22,000-$66,000**

### Wave 3 (July 7): Final Reorder
- Top up any sizes/colors running low
- Order for remaining expected demand
- **Must arrive at printer by July 10 to meet production deadline**
