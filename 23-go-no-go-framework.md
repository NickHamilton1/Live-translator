# Go / No-Go Decision Framework

---

## Decision Point #1: After Legal Review (April 18)

### KILL the project if:
- Attorney says green-zone designs still carry significant trademark risk (i.e., generic spider imagery + movie-night context is enough for Disney to win a claim)
- The legal risk at 100K+ unit scale is categorically different from small Etsy sellers (it is — visibility invites enforcement)
- There's no viable design concept that evokes Spider-Man fandom without touching any IP
- Estimated legal exposure exceeds projected profit in the base case scenario

### PIVOT the design approach if:
- Some concepts are safe but others aren't — narrow the line to only the safest designs
- Attorney recommends specific changes (e.g., "drop all spider imagery, keep only text-based nostalgia designs")
- Need to rebrand from "spider-themed" to "superhero-movie-night-themed" to stay safe
- Some yellow-zone concepts must become red-zone and get cut

### PROCEED as planned if:
- Attorney confirms green-zone designs are low-risk
- The design guardrails are sufficient to avoid likely enforcement action
- Risk is manageable with the LLC structure and legal contingency fund
- At least 10 of 15 concepts survive legal review

### Minimum viable version (if legal picture is restrictive):
- **Pure movie-night event merch** with zero spider/web imagery
- "Opening Night Crew," "Since 2002," "Three Eras" — text-only designs that reference the cultural moment without any character-adjacent imagery
- Lower revenue potential (weaker emotional hook) but near-zero legal risk
- Likely reduces target from 100K units to 10-30K (still profitable at conservative scenario)

## Decision Point #2: Pre-Launch Readiness (June 1)

### KILL or DELAY if:
- Shopify store not functional
- Meta Business Manager not verified (can't scale ads)
- Blanks not ordered or delayed (won't arrive in time)
- Virginia printer can't confirm capacity/timeline
- No viable fulfillment path identified

### PROCEED if:
- Store is live and tested (end-to-end purchase flow works)
- Meta account verified and pixel firing
- Blanks ordered and on track to arrive by June 14
- Printer confirmed capacity and pricing
- At least 10 designs ready with ad creatives produced

---

## Decision Point #3: CPA Validation (June 23, 7 Days After Launch)

### KILL or SCALE BACK if:
- Blended CPA > $10 after 1,000+ clicks — unit economics don't work
- Conversion rate < 1% — product-market fit issue (designs don't resonate, price too high, or audience wrong)
- Zero purchases from any audience — fundamental problem
- Meta ad account restricted or disabled

### SCALE CAUTIOUSLY if:
- Blended CPA $8–$10 — profitable but thin. Focus on optimizing before increasing spend.
- Conversion rate 1–2% — room for improvement. Test new creatives, adjust pricing, try bundles.
- Only 1-2 audiences working — narrow focus, don't diversify yet.

### SCALE AGGRESSIVELY if:
- Blended CPA < $6 — model is working. Increase budget 20% every 3 days.
- Conversion rate > 3% — strong product-market fit.
- Multiple audiences performing — diversify and scale.
- Bundle AOV > $35 — customers buying more than one shirt.

---

## Decision Point #4: Mid-Campaign (July 7)

### PAUSE SCALING if:
- CPA has risen above $10 (creative fatigue, audience saturation)
- Printer is behind schedule (can't fulfill what you've sold)
- Customer complaints about quality, sizing, or delivery estimates
- Legal notice received (see `04-risk-scenarios.md` for response plan)

### CONTINUE if:
- CPA stable at $6-8
- Printer on schedule
- Customer satisfaction high (low refund rate, positive social mentions)
- No legal issues

---

## The One-Sentence Test

> **If we can't honestly answer "this is original spider-themed movie merch, not Spider-Man merch" when asked, the project's legal foundation is broken.**

Every design, every ad, every product listing must pass this test. If the business model only works by implicitly trading on Marvel's IP, it doesn't work.

## Financial Kill Switches

| Metric | Threshold | Action |
|---|---|---|
| CPA > $12 for 5+ days | — | Pause all prospecting. Retarget only. |
| Total ad spend > $50K with < 3,000 orders | — | CPA > $16. Kill the campaign. |
| Refund rate > 10% | — | Product quality issue. Pause ads, fix product. |
| Legal notice received | — | Pause ads for affected designs. Comply. Assess. |
| Shopify store disabled | — | Emergency: migrate to backup store or wind down. |
| Stripe/payment freeze | — | Cannot process orders. Pause everything until resolved. |
